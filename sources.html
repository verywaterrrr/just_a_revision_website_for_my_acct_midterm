What these lessons use

Learning objective

Prepare for the ACCT2010 midterm by understanding and applying the concepts in the supplied guide, practising independently and explaining the reasoning. These teaching pages are self-contained revision materials. They are an independent study resource and are not an official HKUST publication or a prediction of exam questions.

Source cautions

  • Chapter 6 PDF pp. 54-55 use inconsistent figures between question and solution. From the stated question, required allowance is $10,000, unadjusted allowance $3,000 credit, and expense $7,000. Lesson 6 explains the calculation.
  • Chapter 3 uses a particular operating presentation for PPE disposal gains/losses. Follow the question's context; avoid assuming every disposal gain belongs in the same subtotal.
  • Chapter 7 uses market/replacement-cost and NRV terminology in different examples. Use the measurement basis expressly supplied in each exercise.
